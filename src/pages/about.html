A gateway between Nepal and the rest of the world.

We are a Nepal-based company that moves cargo out of the country and helps international buyers find the products they want to bring in from it.

Company overview

Nepal Shipping & Sourcing Pvt. Ltd. is a private limited company in Nepal, established in 2018. We work with exporters, importers and international buyers who need goods moved to or from Nepal, or who need help finding reliable producers here.

Our work combines two things that are often handled by separate firms: freight coordination and product sourcing. Keeping them together means the person who checks your goods at the workshop is part of the same team that books them onto a flight or a container.

Our logo carries the line Your Gateway to Nepal. It describes the role we aim to play for clients: a dependable, local point of contact for trade in and out of the country.

Our mission

To make trade with Nepal straightforward for businesses everywhere, by coordinating shipments carefully, sourcing products honestly and communicating clearly at every step.

Our vision

A Nepal whose producers reach international markets with confidence, and whose buyers abroad can rely on a well-run local partner to get goods from workshop to destination.

Our values

The principles behind how we quote, plan and report on every job.

  • Honesty

    We describe services, timelines and costs as they are. If something depends on a carrier, an agent or a regulator, we say so.

  • Care

    Handmade goods and natural products need careful packing and handling. We treat each consignment as if it were our own.

  • Responsiveness

    Questions get answers, and problems get flagged early, while there is still time to act on them.

  • Fairness

    We aim for arrangements that work for buyers and for the Nepalese producers who supply them.

  • Accountability

    One named contact owns your inquiry from the first message until the goods arrive.

Our approach to shipping and sourcing

Two services, one way of working: plan carefully, check early and keep you informed.

Shipping

We start from the cargo and the deadline, not from a fixed product. Weight, volume, value and urgency decide whether air, sea or road makes sense, and which border crossing or gateway suits the route. We then coordinate carriers, agents and documents so each leg hands over cleanly to the next.

Sourcing

We start from your specification. We look for producers who can meet it, verify that they are who they say they are, and arrange samples before any order is placed. During production we follow up regularly, and before dispatch we check goods against the approved sample.

Our commitment to customer service

Cross-border shipments involve many hands. Our job is to make sure you never have to chase them yourself.

  • We acknowledge every inquiry and tell you what we need to prepare a quote.
  • Quotes are written, itemised and clear about what could change.
  • You receive updates at agreed milestones, not only when something goes wrong.
  • If a plan needs to change, we explain why and set out your options.
