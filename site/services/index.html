Air freight

The fastest way out of a landlocked country. Air freight suits samples, urgent orders, perishables and high-value goods where transit time matters more than cost per kilo.

What we coordinate

  • Space booking with airlines and cargo agents from Kathmandu
  • Chargeable-weight checks so you know the real cost up front
  • Air waybill and export documents
  • Labelling and airline-compliant packing

Well suited to

  • Samples and small consignments
  • Time-critical orders
  • Light, high-value goods

Sea freight

For larger volumes, ocean freight is usually the most economical choice. Cargo travels overland from Nepal to an Indian seaport and onward by ocean carrier, so the road and sea legs need to be planned together.

What we coordinate

  • Full container (FCL) and shared container (LCL) options
  • Inland haulage from Nepal to the port
  • Transit documentation for the journey through India
  • Ocean booking with carriers or consolidators

Well suited to

  • Bulk and palletised cargo
  • Regular, planned orders
  • Heavy or bulky goods

Ask about sea freight

Land transport

Road transport connects producers across Nepal to airports, seaports and neighbouring markets. We arrange trucking for collection, domestic movement and cross-border delivery.

What we coordinate

  • Collection from suppliers and workshops
  • Domestic trucking within Nepal
  • Cross-border movements through land border points
  • Feeder transport to ports and airports

Well suited to

  • Consolidating goods from several suppliers
  • Shipments to India and the region
  • The first leg of sea freight

Ask about land transport

Freight forwarding

A shipment from Nepal often involves a trucker, a customs agent, a port or airport handler and an international carrier. As your forwarder, we bring those parties together and act as your single point of contact.

What we coordinate

  • Route and mode planning
  • Bookings across each leg of the journey
  • Consolidation of smaller consignments
  • Milestone updates from pickup to arrival

Well suited to

  • First-time exporters
  • Multi-supplier orders
  • Buyers managing shipments from abroad

Ask about freight forwarding

Import and export assistance

Missing or inconsistent paperwork is one of the most common causes of delay. We help you work out which documents your goods and destination need, and help prepare them correctly.

What we help with

  • Commercial invoices and packing lists
  • Identifying certificates your goods may need, such as certificates of origin
  • Checking documents are consistent before submission
  • Guidance on destination requirements

Well suited to

  • New exporters and importers
  • Food, herbal and regulated goods
  • Buyers unfamiliar with Nepal’s procedures

Requirements depend on the product and destination country. We will tell you what applies to your shipment when we quote.

Ask about import and export

Customs coordination

Customs clearance is filed by licensed agents. We coordinate with them on your behalf so declarations, supporting documents and inspections are handled without loose ends.

What we coordinate

  • Document hand-over to licensed customs agents
  • Follow-up on queries and inspections
  • Clearance status updates

Well suited to

  • Export clearance in Nepal
  • Shipments with several documents

Ask about customs coordination

Cargo handling and packing

Handmade and natural goods are easily damaged in transit. We coordinate export-grade packing, marking and loading so cargo arrives in the condition it left.

What we coordinate

  • Export packing suited to the mode of transport
  • Carton marking and labelling
  • Palletising and loading supervision

Well suited to

  • Fragile crafts and ceramics
  • Textiles and felt goods
  • Mixed consignments

Ask about cargo handling
