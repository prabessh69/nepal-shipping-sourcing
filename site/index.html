A Nepal-based partner for moving goods, and for finding them.

Nepal is landlocked, and most of its trade depends on careful coordination: airline capacity out of Kathmandu, trucking to the border, transit through India and onward booking from the seaports. Paperwork, timing and packing all have to line up.

We work on both sides of that problem. For shippers, we coordinate freight and documents from pickup to departure and beyond. For international buyers, we help locate Nepalese producers, check what they make and bring the goods to the point of export.

Shipping and freight services

Each shipment is planned around the cargo, the deadline and the budget, then booked with carriers and agents suited to the route.

  • Product Sourcing

    Finding and verifying Nepalese producers, approving samples and checking goods before export.

  • Air Freight

    Time-sensitive and high-value consignments routed through Tribhuvan International Airport.

  • Sea Freight

    Full and part container loads moved by road to Indian seaports, then onward by ocean carrier.

  • Land Transport

    Trucking within Nepal and across the border, including feeder legs to ports and airports.

  • Freight Forwarding

    One point of contact for booking, consolidation and coordination across every leg of the journey.

  • Import and Export Assistance

    Help preparing commercial invoices, packing lists and the certificates your goods need.

  • Customs Coordination

    Working with licensed customs agents so declarations are complete and filed on time.

  • Cargo Handling, Packaging and Branding

    Export-grade packing, branded retail packaging, labels and carton marking.

  • Quality Check and Control

    Pre-shipment inspection against your approved sample, with photo and video reports.

Sourcing in Nepal, with someone on the ground.

Buying from small producers abroad is hard to manage remotely. We help you find the right workshop, confirm it can deliver, and keep watch until the goods are packed and on their way.

How sourcing works

Supplier identification
Shortlisting producers that match your product, volume and standards.
Supplier verification
Checking registration, capacity and track record before you commit.
Samples
Requesting, collecting and dispatching samples for your approval.
Quality inspection
Checking goods against your approved sample and specification.
Production follow-up
Regular updates on progress so delays surface early.
Packing and export
Export-ready packing, documents and freight booked in one flow.
Branding and packaging
Custom labels, hang-tags, branded boxes and export-ready retail packaging, arranged at the source.

How we work with clients

Practical commitments you can hold us to on every inquiry and every shipment.

  • One accountable contact

    You deal with one person who knows your shipment, not a different desk for every question.

  • Clear quotes before booking

    We set out what is included, what depends on third parties and what could change, before anything is booked.

  • Updates at each milestone

    Pickup, export clearance, departure and arrival: you hear from us at each stage, in writing.

  • Straight answers

    If a route, product or timeline is not realistic, we tell you early and suggest an alternative.

From first message to delivery

The same five steps apply whether you are shipping your own goods or asking us to source them.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Product, quantity, origin, destination and timing. Rough figures are fine to start.

  2. Plan and quotation

    We recommend a route and mode, list the documents required and send a written quote.

  3. Sourcing and checks

    For sourcing orders, we identify suppliers, arrange samples and inspect goods before dispatch.

  4. Booking and documents

    Space is booked with carriers, and export paperwork is prepared with the relevant agents.

  5. Dispatch and updates

    Cargo departs and you receive milestone updates until it reaches the agreed destination.
