How sourcing works

Each step can be used on its own, but most buyers ask us to take an order from first search through to shipment.

  1. Supplier identification

    We translate your specification into a shortlist of producers whose products, capacity and pricing fit what you need.

  2. Supplier verification

    Before you commit, we check business registration and look at production capacity, and wherever possible we visit the workshop in person.

  3. Product sourcing

    We collect quotations, compare options with you and help agree specifications, quantities and lead times in writing.

  4. Sample coordination

    We arrange samples, review them against your brief and ship them to you for approval.

  5. Production monitoring

    Once an order is placed, we follow up with the producer regularly and report progress, so any delay is visible early.

  6. Quality inspection

    Before dispatch, we check finished goods against the approved sample and your specification, and share findings with photos.

  7. Packaging coordination

    We make sure goods are packed for the journey, with carton marks and labels that meet your requirements.

  8. Export and shipping

    Our freight team arranges documents, booking and transport, so sourcing and shipping run as one job.

Some products, especially food, herbs and plant materials, need export permits, certificates or laboratory tests. We will tell you what applies to your product and destination before an order is placed.

What to send us to start

The more detail you share, the faster we can come back with suppliers and prices. Rough answers are fine.

  • Product description, with photos or drawings
  • Materials, sizes and colours
  • Quantity per order, and how often you will reorder
  • Target price range, if you have one
  • Labelling, packaging or certification needs
  • Destination country and preferred delivery date
